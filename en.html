Privacy Policy

Kingyo (Goldfish Scooping) / Last updated: November 9, 2026

日本語版

Katsumi Sakurai ("the developer") provides this policy for the iOS app "きんぎょすくい" / Kingyo ("the app").

1. Who collects what

The app has no account, no sign-in and no server operated by the developer. The developer never receives any information about you. The app does show ads, however, and Google, as the advertising provider, does collect information (see section 3).

The app does not use or collect your name, e-mail address, phone number, contacts, GPS location, photos, camera or microphone. Game records are never sent anywhere.

2. What is stored on your device

Only the following is stored on the device itself (iOS UserDefaults). None of it leaves the device.

ItemContent
Best scoreYour highest score
Top 10The ten highest scores and when they were made
Guide flagWhether the first-launch "how to play" screen has been shown

Deleting the app removes all of it. The developer cannot read or retrieve it.

3. Advertising

The app uses Google AdMob, a third-party advertising service, to show three kinds of ads.

TypeWhere it appears
BannerOne banner at the top of the screen at all times
InterstitialWhen a game ends, and only if enough time has passed since the previous one
RewardedOnly when you tap "watch an ad for one more poi" on the game over screen

The rewarded ad plays only when you choose it yourself. If you decline it you can start a new game at any time with "play again" (to continue the same game you either watch the ad or exchange 100 coins you have collected). If no rewarded ad can be loaded, for example with no network, no ad is shown and you simply stay on the same screen without the extra poi; the same happens if you close the ad early. Nothing is ever charged. When no banner ad can be shown, that space displays this app's own name and a goldfish drawing instead, which is not a third-party advertisement.

To deliver and measure ads and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect the following, as stated in Google's own data disclosure:

TypeContent
Device IDIDFA and identifiers issued by AdMob
Advertising dataImpressions, taps and similar events
Product interactionApp launches and interactions around the ads
Coarse locationCountry or region estimated from the IP address (no GPS)
DiagnosticsCrashes, request outcomes, timing data

This information is collected and used by Google; the developer does not receive its contents. For how Google handles, retains and deletes it, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and Google privacy & security. The developer only embeds third parties that protect this information to an equivalent standard.

Your scores and records are never passed to the advertising service.

4. App Tracking Transparency (ATT)

On first launch iOS asks whether you allow tracking.

Your choiceWhat happens
AllowAds may be personalised using the IDFA
Ask app not to trackThe IDFA is not included in ad requests. Ads are still shown, but are not based on interests tracked across apps and websites

Either way the whole game remains playable. You can change the choice at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking, including withdrawing permission you gave earlier; from that moment the IDFA is no longer used.

The app requests no ad at all, not even the banner, until the tracking prompt has been answered.

5. In-app purchases

There are none. Scooping goldfish earns in-game "coins" that can be exchanged for another poi (the paper scoop), but they are simply part of the score for that single game: they have no relation to real money, they do not carry over to the next game, and they cannot be bought, won as a prize or cashed out.

6. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13.

7. Changes to this policy

This policy may change as the law or the app changes. Significant changes will be announced on this page.

8. Deleting your information

Everything stored on the device disappears when you delete the app. For information Google has collected for advertising, see Google My Ad Center, or withdraw permission in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. For anything else, please contact the developer below.
